Welcome to your medical home.

We've got you covered.

Katakwi General Hospital is a public healthcare facility located in Eastern Uganda.
We provide affordable, quality healthcare to the people of Katakwi District and surrounding areas.
We are situated in the central business district of Katakwi town, approximately 52km northeast of Soroti
Regional Hospital and 118km southwest of Moroto Regional Referral Hospital, serving Katakwi and neighboring
districts such as Amuria, Kapelebyong, Kumi, Nakapiripirit, and Napak.
Our emergency response team is always ready to handle critical cases with speed and skill.
Your healthis in safe hands — 24/7 care when you need it most!

Our Mission: To provide patient-centered healthcare with access to quality services
Our Vision: A Healthy, Productive and Prosperous Community

Medicine

Expert Medicine Services

Comprehensive medical care for all age groups. High-quality diagnostic and treatment services. Experienced and dedicated medical staff. Accessible and affordable healthcare for the community

From general consultations and chronic disease management (like diabetes, hypertension, and infections) to emergency care and inpatient treatment in our male, female, maternity, and children’s wards — our dedicated medical team provides comprehensive internal medicine and outpatient services.

Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for all age groups.
Smile with confidence! Our dental unit offers essential oral health care, including check-ups, extractions, fillings, scaling & polishing, and basic restorative treatments. We focus on preventive care to keep your teeth and gums healthy for life. Whether it’s a toothache or routine dental hygiene, we provide gentle, professional dentistry right here in Katakwi — no need to travel far!

Pharmacy

Access quality, affordable medicines without delay. Our well-stocked pharmacy dispenses essential drugs, prescriptions from our doctors, and over-the-counter medications under strict quality control. We ensure you get the right medication, with clear guidance on usage. Convenient, trusted pharmacy services — because proper treatment starts with the right medicines!

Laboratory

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. Our laboratory performs a wide range of essential tests including blood work, malaria screening, HIV testing, urinalysis, and other routine clinical analyses. With a spacious waiting area and dedicated staff, we deliver fast, reliable results to support timely medical decisions. Science you can trust — precise lab services supporting better health outcomes for you and your family.

Our mission is to improve the health of our communities by providing a high level of preventive care.

Affordable & Government-Supported Care — Quality services at minimal or no cost for many essential treatments. Patient-Centered Approach — Friendly staff, clean environment, and ongoing improvements including new operating theatres and infrastructure. Serving the Community — We care for thousands of patients every year with dedication and professionalism.

We provide the exceptional service we'd want to experience ourselves!
