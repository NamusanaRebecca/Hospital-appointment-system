We've got you covered.

Katakwi General Hospital is a public healthcare facility located in Eastern Uganda.
We provide affordable, quality healthcare to the people of Katakwi District and surrounding areas.
We are situated in the central business district of Katakwi town, approximately 52km northeast of Soroti
Regional Hospital and 118km southwest of Moroto Regional Referral Hospital, serving Katakwi and neighboring
districts such as Amuria, Kapelebyong, Kumi, Nakapiripirit, and Napak.

Our Mission: To provide patient-centered healthcare with access to quality services
Our Vision: A Healthy, Productive and Prosperous Community
